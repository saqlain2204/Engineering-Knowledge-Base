How the guides are written

Built to make things click

  • From zeroEvery term is defined in plain words before it is used. Short sentences. No hand-waving.
  • Worked by handScheduling charts, address translations, attention scores and latency budgets are solved step by step and checked with code.
  • Measured, not guessedNumbers come from real labs: timings, fault counts and p99s you can reproduce on your own machine.
  • Kept currentPages are checked against recent releases, from Kubernetes versions to new LLM serving tricks.
