AI Engineering Learning Notes

Concepts, how things work, pipelines, and engineering tradeoffs.

Suggested reading order

New to machine learning? This path goes from the first model to LLM apps, each page building on the one before. Every page also stands on its own, and the pages on this path have Previous / Next links at the bottom. The full topic list is below.

  1. Classical ML from Zero: what a model is and how it learns
  2. Trees & Ensembles: the strongest tools for tables
  3. Unsupervised Learning: finding structure with no labels
  4. Foundations: neural networks, loss and optimizers
  5. Transformers: attention and the blocks inside every LLM
  6. Tokenization: how text becomes numbers
  7. LLMs: large models, scaling and their lifecycle
  8. Decoding & Sampling: how the next token is picked
  9. Pretraining: learning from huge amounts of text
  10. Fine-Tuning: adapting a model with SFT and LoRA
  11. Post-Training: RLHF, DPO and alignment
  12. Embeddings: meaning as vectors
  13. RAG: answering from your own documents
  14. Agents & Tool Use: models that take actions
  15. Inference Optimization: serving fast and cheap
  16. Evaluation: knowing whether it works
