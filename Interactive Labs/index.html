Interactive Labs

Hard ideas get easy when you can poke them. Each lab runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no install. Change a number, watch what happens, then read the guide behind it.

AI engineering

How language models think

  • Self-Attention, Step by Step Pick a word and follow it through query-key scores, scaling, the causal mask, softmax and the weighted sum. Includes a live attention heatmap. TransformersQ, K, V
  • How an LLM Picks the Next Token Move temperature, top-k, top-p and min-p. See which tokens survive, how the odds shift, and sample 500 times to check. DecodingSampling

Operating systems

Inside the kernel

  • CPU Scheduling Simulator Edit processes and compare FCFS, SJF, SRTF, Round Robin and Priority with a Gantt chart and waiting times. Gantt chartRound Robin
  • Page Replacement: FIFO vs LRU vs OPT Step through page requests with three policies side by side, read why each victim was chosen, and find Belady's anomaly. Virtual memoryLRU

Networks and systems

Traffic and addresses

  • Load Balancing Simulator Add a slow server and compare round robin, random, least connections and power of two choices on p99 latency. p99Queues
  • Subnet Calculator, Bit by Bit See the network, broadcast and host range of any IPv4 address, bit by bit, and split a network into smaller subnets. CIDRIPv4

How the labs are built

Every lab is one plain HTML page with a little JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server, and the math is checked against the worked examples in the guides (for example, the classic 20-request page string gives 15, 12 and 9 faults for FIFO, LRU and OPT with 3 frames). Each lab works on phones and in both light and dark themes.

Want a lab on another topic? Open an issue on GitHub.
