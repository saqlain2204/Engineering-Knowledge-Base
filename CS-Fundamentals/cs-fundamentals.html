How these pages are written

  • From zero: every term is defined in plain words before it is used.
  • Worked by hand: scheduling charts, address translations, and latency budgets are solved step by step, and the numbers are checked with small simulators.
  • Connected to production: each page ends with real scenarios, such as container CPU throttling, double-booked seats, frozen services, slow APIs, full disks, overlapping networks, and errors during deploys.
